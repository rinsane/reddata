Know what this does before you use it.

It subscribes your account by driving reddit.com directly, because Reddit closed self-service API keys in November 2025 and there is no approved app behind this page. That is outside Reddit's sanctioned API and against the letter of their terms, so there is some risk to your account. It only ever subscribes — never posts, votes, comments, or reads messages — and it joins in batches with a pause between each.
